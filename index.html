Our Story

Trends and Curves is a women-led kurti store in Yendada, Visakhapatnam. What started as a small shop with a passion for good fabric and honest pricing has become a go-to spot for women across the city who want quality without the boutique markup.

We serve working women, homemakers, students, and anyone who believes that dressing well should not mean overspending. Every piece in the store is selected with care, and we are always happy to help you find something that fits your style and your day.
